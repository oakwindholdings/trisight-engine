# How This Study Works — and How to Refute It

*Prepared for Dick O'Leary · Oakwind strategy estate · August 2026*

## What we measured

For each sealed strategy we asked two questions with one method:

1. **What did the backtest claim?** Taken verbatim from the strategy's own lockdown/seal documents, with file citations.
2. **What did the paper ledger actually record?** Taken from the production fills ledgers, with pull dates and citations.

Where both sides could be stated in the same units, we computed the ratio — the **inflation factor**. Where they could not, we **refused** and said exactly why. A refusal is a finding, not a gap: it means the claim cannot currently be checked against reality, which is itself the problem this study exists to expose.

## The two dimensions

- **Return inflation** (claimed %/yr ÷ realized %/yr) — computable only when both sides state a rate over a dated window of at least 60 days. **Zero of ten strategies passed that bar.** The reasons are itemized per strategy: ledgers with corrupted cost bases, claims that decline to state a calendar window, records never aggregated, and one strategy that has never produced a single fill.
- **Win-rate inflation** (claimed hit rate ÷ realized hit rate) — computable when the realized side has ≥30 closed trades. Four strategies qualified:

| Strategy | Claimed | Realized | Inflation |
|---|---|---|---|
| High 5 | 92.3% (n=5,321) | 45.1% (n=122) | **2.05×** |
| Automated Swing | 92.2% (n=2,107) | 56.7% (n=104)* | **1.63×** |
| Escalator Reclaimed Shadow | 63.0% (n=349) | 45.3% (n=150) | **1.39×** |
| Oakwind Swing Trader | 67.7% (n=15,028) | 58.7% (n=254) | **1.15×** |

*\*37 of Automated Swing's 104 realized round-trips were later ruled fabricated phantom exits and voided — the realized figure is itself unreliable, in the claim's favor.*

## Structural context you should hold while reading any page

A prior fleet-wide audit found **every** sealed backtest ranked its picks inside a frozen, survivor-biased universe: companies that died or delisted during the test period were erased from history before the test began. This can only inflate results, and it hits dip-buying strategies (both Oakwinds) hardest — they are precisely the strategies that would have caught the falling knives the frozen list deletes.

## How to refute any page

Every number carries either an estate file citation (open the file, check the quote) or a content-addressed record hash (append-only — nothing can be silently edited). If you believe a better claim document or a cleaner realized ledger exists, name the file: the record set takes corrections as *supersessions*, with the original kept visible. If any excerpt is misquoted, one file citation is enough to overturn the line it supports.

## What this does NOT say

This study does not say the strategy *ideas* are bad. It says the *evidence* offered for them cannot support the claims made — and in four measurable cases, the claims overstated reality by 1.15× to 2.05× on hit rate alone, before the survivorship bias is priced in. The separate Phase-3 exercise (135 pre-registered candidate variants, all run through an identical honest gauntlet: **zero survived** adversarial review) shows what the bar for a real claim now looks like.
